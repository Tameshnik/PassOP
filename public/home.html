How PassOp works

1. Create your vault

Pick a strong master password. We derive a key using modern KDFs to encrypt everything on‑device.

2. Add your logins

Save sites, usernames, and notes. Use the generator to craft unique credentials for each account.

3. Use anywhere

Access on the web app or desktop build. Optional cloud sync keeps devices in step without sharing secrets.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What happens if I forget my master password?

For maximum privacy, there is no backdoor. Enable a recovery key and store it safely offline.

Can I sync across devices?

Yes — optional sync can be added later. Your vault stays encrypted end‑to‑end.

Is PassOp open source?

You can expose parts of PassOp as open source or keep it private — this template is ready either way.
